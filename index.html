Hacker News, in order.

An independent recreation of hckr news, originally created by Wayne Larsen, with dark mode. Stories are listed by when they appeared on the original feed. Orange points mark stories currently on the HN homepage.

The feed is updated every 30 minutes. Refresh fetches current points and comments from the official Hacker News API. Top filters select the highest-scoring stories for each day, keeping them in chronological order.
